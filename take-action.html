Every action here is real: a comment that lands on the public record, a message that reaches the right inbox, a story that gets heard. Ranked by leverage — start at the top.

#1 · 5–15 minutes · closes Aug 4 at 4 pm

Comment on the three protected-area proposals

The province is collecting public feedback on the Meziadin, the Klappan, and Dene K'éh Kusān right now. We've done the annoying part — the hub takes you straight to the right page and the right inbox for each one. If you work, guide, hunt, or fish this country, say so. That's what gets weighed.

Start with any of the three

#2 · 5 minutes · your words carry weight

Send an original message to decision-makers

A form letter is counted; an original message is read. Each area page has a message-writer that reaches the BC Ministry of Water, Land and Resource Stewardship. Two honest sentences beat an essay.

PROTECT THE SOURCE. KEEP THE COUNTRY OPEN.

The vast and mountainous expanse of northern British Columbia is home to some of the most important headwaters on the continent — the Skeena, the Nass, the Stikine, the Taku, and the Liard. Northern Headwaters is a common-sense effort to protect these living systems while keeping the country open, the water clean, and the jobs long-lasting.
