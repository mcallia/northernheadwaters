The framework

Three zones, clearly drawn

The plan behind the proposals is simple enough to say in one breath: protect the sources, steward the living country, keep working lands working — with rules everyone can see.

Zone · Protected

The sources

Salmon nurseries, spawning grounds, critical winter ranges, cultural heartlands. No industrial disturbance — and hunting, fishing and recreation access stays.

Zone · Stewardship

The living country

Cultural use, guardian programs, restoration, and low-impact economies — guiding, tourism, trapping — managed with the Nations who know it best.

Zone · Working

The working lands

Mining, forestry and energy in defined footprints, to best-practice standards, with monitoring that means something — certainty for workers and investors alike.

The three current proposals are the first pieces of the protected zone. What's protected protects what's working: clean water and healthy runs are the North's longest-running paycheque.

The values on the line

What these places actually hold

Not scenery — inventory. The things Northerners name when they explain why this country matters.

Sockeye, chinook & steelhead

The Meziadin runs, the Skeena's steelhead water, the Stikine's kings — food, income, identity.

Moose & caribou country

Seven caribou herds range the Kaska plan area; the moose draw sustains families across the North.

Volcanoes & obsidian

Mount Edziza — Ice Mountain — armed ten millennia of trade with volcanic glass. Before Rome, there was the Stikine.

Berry grounds & cultural sites

Harvest slopes, trails, camps and villages — law, memory and food carried across generations.

Lodges & guiding

World-class steelhead lodges and guide territories that bring visitors' dollars into northern towns.

Recreation that lasts

Sledding, hunting, fishing, riding, hiking — the access the conservancies keep open.

Grizzly & goat ranges

23,000+ ha of mountain-goat winter range in the Meziadin plan area alone.

Spawning grounds

Glacier-fed creeks — 60.8 km of them in the Meziadin plan area — where next decade's runs begin.
