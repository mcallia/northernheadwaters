Resources & organizations

Who does what in the headwaters

The governments, agencies, planning portals and organizations doing real work in this country. We're not all the same, and we don't speak for each other — we share the watersheds. Listing here isn't an endorsement; it's a map of who to talk to.

First Nations governments & institutions

Stikine · Klappan

Tahltan Central Government

The Tahltan Nation's central government — co-author of the Klappan Plan and the province's first Declaration Act consent agreements.

Upper Nass

Gitanyow Hereditary Chiefs

Hereditary governance of the Gitanyow Lax'yip — joint proponents of the Meziadin salmon-habitat conservation plan.

Nass

Nisg̱a'a Lisims Government

The treaty government of the Nisg̱a'a Nation — joint proponent of the Meziadin plan, and a major player in the region's economy.

Liard · Kechika

Kaska Dena Council

The Kaska Dena's collective voice in BC — behind Dene K'éh Kusān, "Always Will Be There."

Liard · Kechika

Dena Kayeh Institute

The Kaska institute leading the Dene K'éh Kusān proposal and the Kaska land guardians program.

Taku

Taku River Tlingit First Nation

Governing the Taku country from Atlin — partner in the Tulsequah Chief cleanup framework.

Provincial processes & agencies

The portal

Planning in Partnership

BC's portal for northwest land-use planning — where the official documents and comment forms live.

Have your say

govTogetherBC

The province's engagement hub — the Klappan and Kaska feedback forms are here until Aug 4.

The estate

BC Parks

Manager of the parks and conservancies the new proposals would join — from Mount Edziza to Tā Ch'ilā.

Conservation, science & law

Salmon

SkeenaWild Conservation Trust

Salmon-first science and stewardship for the Skeena — where salmon thrive, all life flourishes.

Hunters & anglers

BC Wildlife Federation

The province's largest conservation organization — the voice of the people who actually use the backcountry.

Transboundary

Salmon Beyond Borders

Fishermen, Tribes and First Nations defending the rivers that feed us — the Taku, Stikine and Unuk on both sides of the border.

Environmental law

West Coast Environmental Law

Legal analysis you can actually read — including the definitive briefs on Bill C-5 and fast-tracking.

Salmon science

Pacific Salmon Foundation

Science and on-the-ground investment in wild Pacific salmon across BC.

The M-K

Muskwa-Kechika Advisory Board

Stewards of the management area that already underlies 92% of the proposed Dene K'éh Kusān.

News from the region

Regional journalism

The Skeena

Independent journalism from the Skeena and BC's northwest — including the Humans of Skeena series.

Investigations

The Narwhal

The deepest reporting on BC mining, land use and Indigenous-led conservation.

On air

CFNR Network

First Nations radio for northern BC — news from the communities these decisions touch.

Know an organization or resource that belongs here? Tell us: info@northernheadwaters.ca. We curate this list for register as much as substance — groups doing real work in the region, speaking to Northerners rather than about them.

The vast and mountainous expanse of northern British Columbia is home to some of the most important headwaters on the continent — the Skeena, the Nass, the Stikine, the Taku, and the Liard. Northern Headwaters is a common-sense effort to protect these living systems while keeping the country open, the water clean, and the jobs long-lasting.
